## Derived requirements and their grounding

These are our requirements, derived from observed structure. They are not claims about vendor behavior.

1. **Separate attendance, clinical completion and billing.** Grounded in a single Status selector carrying lifecycle, physical presence, clinical completion, financial completion and exception meanings at once ([REFERENCE-STATE-VOCABULARIES.md](REFERENCE-STATE-VOCABULARIES.md)). One field cannot express "arrived, treated, not yet billed" without losing a fact, which is why the invariant in [AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md) is written as three separate facts.
2. **Keep automatic confirmation distinct from manual confirmation.** Grounded in `Confirmed` and `Confirmed (auto)` appearing as separate values; without the distinction a reminder policy cannot be measured ([REFERENCE-STATE-VOCABULARIES.md](REFERENCE-STATE-VOCABULARIES.md)).
3. **Expose conflicts and requests as inspectable objects.** Grounded in Appointment Conflicts and Appointment Requests being separate scheduler surfaces rather than errors inside a save path ([REFERENCE-DOMAIN-OBSERVATIONS.md](REFERENCE-DOMAIN-OBSERVATIONS.md)).
4. **Give every workflow screen an addressable route.** Grounded in six overlay screens that open without changing the URL, so they cannot be linked, revisited by back/forward, or cited in support ([REFERENCE-WORKFLOW-MAP.md](REFERENCE-WORKFLOW-MAP.md)).
5. **Carry the tenant in the credential and keep the credential out of the path.** Grounded in the session token travelling as a URL path segment and a clinic id supplied by the caller on nearly every request ([REFERENCE-API-OBSERVATIONS.md](REFERENCE-API-OBSERVATIONS.md)). The untested question of whether a token is rejected for a clinic it does not cover is the negative cross-tenant case the readiness gates already require.
6. **Use HTTP status honestly and define one error contract.** Grounded in 46 of the 50 captured API responses carrying a two-key `{status, data}` envelope inside HTTP 200, so failure is not signalled by the transport ([REFERENCE-API-OBSERVATIONS.md](REFERENCE-API-OBSERVATIONS.md)).
7. **Drive audit from an entity registry, and audit command-like events.** Grounded in an enumerated record list selected by action, record and actor, one entry of which is statement generation rather than a record write ([REFERENCE-DOMAIN-OBSERVATIONS.md](REFERENCE-DOMAIN-OBSERVATIONS.md)).
8. **Resolve payer identity instead of storing payer strings.** Grounded in near-duplicate carrier rows for the same payer within a single practice ([REFERENCE-STATE-VOCABULARIES.md](REFERENCE-STATE-VOCABULARIES.md)).
9. **Model treatment at three levels, and the four financial facts separately.** Grounded in treatment, treatment detail and treatment item detail as distinct audited entities, and in bulk check, statement, payment plan and claim being distinct screens with distinct lifecycles ([REFERENCE-DOMAIN-OBSERVATIONS.md](REFERENCE-DOMAIN-OBSERVATIONS.md), [REFERENCE-API-OBSERVATIONS.md](REFERENCE-API-OBSERVATIONS.md)).
