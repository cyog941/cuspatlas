When a payment's funds are held by more than one patient of the charge's merge set (after a merge or transfer), funds are consumed in patient-reference order. A FIFO or oldest-charge-first auto-allocation is a later standing-policy decision (A/F automation authority) | recommended-pending-billing-approval | billing reviewer; practice manager |

Accounts: `patient_receivable` (patient/guarantor responsibility, per charge), `insurer_receivable` (per charge and synthetic payer reference until B5 coverage exists), `production_revenue` (per charge), `unapplied_credit` (per payment and holding patient), `undeposited_funds` (received, not settled, per payment), `settled_cash` (settled, per payment). Sign invariants per dimension (receivables never below zero, unapplied credit never a debit, cash accounts never below zero) are checked by the domain under row locks and again by the deferred trigger | recommended-pending-billing-approval | accounting reviewer |
| M-7 | Correction | Nothing posted is edited or deleted: the runtime role has SELECT and INSERT only and a trigger refuses UPDATE/DELETE for every role that cannot disable triggers. A reversal that would break a sign invariant is refused and names the dependent entries to reverse first (`allocation_reversal_required`, `dependent_reversal_required`, `settlement_reversal_required`) | recommended-pending-billing-approval | accounting reviewer |

## Consequences of the alternatives

- Floats or decimal strings: cent drift and locale-dependent parsing; refused structurally.
- Silent currency coercion: a EUR payment counted as USD; refused with a typed reason.
- "Assign residue to the largest line" or "refuse residue": the first is order-sensitive in a less explainable way when lines tie, the second makes ordinary splits fail; largest remainder with a lower-index tie-break is deterministic and explainable.
- Editing posted rows: no reconstruction of what a statement or claim was based on; refused in the database.
- Relinking posted financial rows on merge: an unmerge would have to edit posted history; read-time resolution avoids that.
