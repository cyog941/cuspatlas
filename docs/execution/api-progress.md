### A4: operation catalog

- `packages/contracts/src/catalog/operations.json` is generated by `scripts/catalog/build-catalog.ts`
  from `ACTION_POLICIES`, the route manifests, the MCP tool table and the worker's literal
  `authorize` calls (`pnpm catalog:generate`); `pnpm catalog:check` (inside `contract:check` and `ci`)
  fails when the committed catalog is stale. 355 operations at this head.
- Each entry: operation id, owner workstream, kind, authorization path, policy action, scope,
  principal kinds, transports (rest/mcp/portal/bridge/worker), REST method/path/schema refs, MCP tool,
  refusal statuses, idempotency, sensitivity (none/operational/phi/phiBulk) and status.
- Authorization paths are recorded honestly: staff REST, MCP and worker operations go through
  `authorize` (ACTION_POLICIES); portal operations through the S5 relationship policy; bridge
  operations through the live device row and allowlist; pairing through the one-time code; health is
  public; five actions are capability-only checks inside another operation. The catalog does not
  claim `authorize` covers portal or device calls.
- Runtime proof: on the real app under the runtime role, every staff REST route (with a scope) called
  by an active member with no scope loads the live membership twice (authentication, then
  `authorize`) and records a `scope_missing` (or `principal_kind_denied` for service-only operations)
  security attempt naming the operation.
- Derived (declared, not independently proven by the catalog): `kind` (GET, read-only MCP tool or a
  read-named POST is a query) and `idempotency` (commands require a key except imaging `unkeyed`
  grants). Per-workstream idempotency suites remain the evidence for the latter.
- Registry reconciliation: `syntheticTasks.read|write|approve` and `outbox.deliver` were used by the
  policy table but missing from SHARED-CONTRACTS; recorded there as F foundation scopes.
