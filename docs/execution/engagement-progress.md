## E1 (portal principals and the grant projection)

Files: `supabase/migrations/20261110000000_portal_principals.sql`, `packages/contracts/src/engagement.schema.json` and `engagement.ts`, `packages/db/src/portal-identity.ts`, `packages/domain/src/engagement/{common,codecs,service,index}.ts`, `packages/domain/src/engagement/portal/{access,principals,session}.ts`, `apps/api/src/routes/{engagement-ops,portal-ops,portal-routes}.ts`, `apps/mcp/src/tools/engagement-ops.ts`, `tests/integration/portal-grants.test.ts`, `tests/integration/engagement-negative-harness.test.ts`, `tests/integration/support/engagement-fixtures.ts`.

Design. A portal principal is a `cuspatlas.principals` row of the new kind `portal` plus a `portal_principals` row binding it to exactly one person record (a patient-identity record of the tenant; S5's counterparty model, so no non-patient person register). It is authenticated by the same EdDSA verifier with a distinct portal audience (`portalIdentity`; the server refuses to boot if it equals the staff audience), and the portal authenticator loads the subject's live enrolment in the token's tenant. The portal context carries principal `portal` and no scopes: no staff action policy admits it, the staff authenticator finds no membership for it, and the database refuses a membership row for a portal principal (trigger, SQLSTATE 42501). The tenant-transaction runner rechecks the live portal enrolment instead of a membership inside every transaction. Enrollment, verification and revocation are staff commands (`portal.principals.enroll` / `verify` / `revoke`, new scope `portal.manage`); invitations of grantees and grant revocation are S5's own `patients.relationships.add` / `revoke` (no second grant command, no grant table, no expiry job). Grants are a projection of S5 relationship rows through `relationshipView` (grantRef = relationshipRef). Acting for another patient calls S5's `evaluatePatientAccess` with the relationship rows share-locked, inside the command's own transaction; acting for the person record itself needs the person to have reached the tenant's age of majority, computed with S5's policy and majority rule (fail closed on a coarse or unknown birth date).

Acceptance cases (`portal-grants.test.ts`, 11 tests, denials under the runtime role):

1. a verified principal is kind `portal`, bound to one person, proofing level `unverified-synthetic`; its session lists the guardian grant with grantRef = relationshipRef, scopes and status, the relationship appears exactly once in the staff S5 list and in the staff `portal.grants.list`; effective access is self plus the child; the marker in names never reaches logs, audit, outbox, attempts or idempotency rows;
2. an unverified principal is pending and its session is 403 with attempt `portal_contact_unverified`;
3. a household phone shared by two patients refuses enrollment (409 `shared_contact_ambiguous`) and creates no principal; a contact that is not on the person's record is 422 `contactNotOnRecord` (contacts are compared as S4 keyed digests, never as plaintext);
4. a request parameter cannot widen a grant (unknown query 400) and two grants for one patient (guardian scheduling + proxy demographics) yield one effective entry with the union of scopes;
5. lowering the tenant age of majority (S5 policy) turns the guardian grant into `expired` / `age_of_majority_reached` and gives the former minor self access; relationship and patient rows are kept;
6. an expired proxy (window ended) and a revoked guardian are listed with S5's status and grant nothing; both rows are kept;
7. revoking a principal denies its existing token at the next request (403 `membership_inactive`), and the principal reads as `revoked`.

Isolation and cross-transport (4 tests): an alpha principal under a beta token is refused exactly like an unknown subject; beta staff get 404 for an alpha principal and an alpha patient's grants; a portal token gets 401 from staff routes and MCP; a staff token gets 401 from the portal; a portal-audience token whose subject is staff is 403 `principal_kind_denied`; a staff-audience token for a portal principal finds no membership (403); the database refuses a staff membership for a portal principal; a portal context handed directly to a staff domain service is 403; one live principal per person (409 `portal_principal_exists`), an unknown person 404, a body carrying `tenantId` 400, and MCP returns the same refusal as REST.

Negative harness (`engagement-negative-harness.test.ts`): 5 staff rows (enroll, verify, revoke, read, grants list), each cross-tenant, revoked and downgraded on REST and MCP with the attempt reason and an unchanged write snapshot; a no-tenant-field check over every body and tool; every portal route against a staff token (401), an MCP token (401), a revoked principal (403), a pending principal (403), another tenant's token (403) and a staff subject on the portal audience (403); every staff E route and tool against a portal token (401). The harness rows were written with E1's implementation in place (they passed on their first run); the E1 suite itself was red first.

## E2 (scoped portal views)

Files: `packages/domain/src/engagement/portal/views.ts`, `apps/api/src/routes/portal-ops.ts` (two routes), `PortalView` and `PortalViewProjection` in `engagement.schema.json`, `tests/integration/portal-views.test.ts`.

Design. `portal.views.read` (`/portal/v1/patients/{patientRef}/views/{view}`) and `portal.views.describe` (`/portal/v1/views/{view}`). A view is a declared allowlist (record fields, item fields of list fields, redaction report) and a relationship-scope requirement taken from the plan's view-to-scope mapping (appointments and requests: `scheduling`; balances: `financial`; documents: `clinical_read`; messages: `consent`; forms: `demographics` + `consent`; demographics: `demographics`). Only declared keys are copied out, in declared order. Producer-backed views: demographics (S4 record groups and the birth date at its precision), appointments (upcoming bookings: reference, window, zone, location label, status; no provider, operatory, creator or request), requests (reference, bounded state `pending` / `scheduled` / `closed`, origin, creation). Views whose producer is not deployed return `coverage: {status: unavailable, reason: producer_not_deployed, owner}` and `fields: null`: documents C15, balances B4, messages E5, forms E4. Authorization runs `requirePortalAccess` (E1) inside the read's transaction; each data-returning read writes a sensitive-read row (operation `portal.views.read`, principal `portal`, the patient reference, query field `view`) in that transaction.

Acceptance cases (`portal-views.test.ts`, 8 tests): (1) for self and guardian, demographics, appointments and requests return exactly the declared key sets at the top level, in `fields` and in every list item, plus the declared redaction list; no tenant id, staff actor id, provider or operatory reference appears; another family member's contact never appears in the child's view; (2) documents, messages, forms (self) and balances (via a guarantor `financial` relationship) are explicit unavailable with owner and null fields; (3) a guardian without `financial`, `clinical_read` or `consent` is 403 `relationship_scope_missing` (attempt row) for balances, documents and forms, and a guarantor has no demographics; (4) an unrelated same-tenant patient, a beta patient and a random reference give byte-identical 404 bodies for two views; (5) a guardian revoked after a successful read is 403 `relationship_revoked` at the next read; (6) an empty appointment list is `available` with `upcoming: []`, distinct from unavailable; (7) a minor reading their own record is 403 `self_below_majority`, an unknown view is 404 and an extra query field is 400; (8) the sensitive-read row names the portal principal and the patient; the marker in names reaches neither logs nor the sensitive-read log.

Negative harness: two portal rows appended (`portal.views.read`, `portal.views.describe`) for the six credential cases.

- Red: `corepack pnpm test -- tests/integration/portal-views.test.ts` before implementation: exit 1, 7 failed / 1 passed (8). The passing case (identical 404 for foreign references) passed vacuously because the route did not exist.
- Green: same command exit 0, 8 passed; harness exit 0, 20 passed. `typecheck`, `lint`, `contract:check` exit 0; `corepack pnpm test` exit 0, 45 files / 992 passed / 13 todo.

Deviations: the plan's documents view returns a short-lived read authorization; with C15 absent it returns explicit unavailable instead. `projectionVersion` is a fixed version string, not a per-deploy allowlist digest.

Unresolved: field sets for balances (B4), documents (C15), messages (E5) and forms (E4); the minimum-necessary field choice for demographics (address and contact values are shown to self and to `demographics` holders) needs a privacy reviewer.

## E3 (portal appointment requests into the shared request aggregate)

Files: `packages/domain/src/engagement/portal/requests.ts`, `PortalRequestSubmit` in `engagement.schema.json`, one portal route, `tests/integration/portal-requests.test.ts`; S seam in `packages/domain/src/scheduling/requests.ts` (`createRequest` gains an optional `PortalRequestAuthority`).

Design. `portal.requests.submit` (`POST /portal/v1/patients/{patientRef}/appointment-requests`) validates a closed portal input (location, optional appointment type, provider preference, preferred windows, flexibility, and the requester's `contactConsent` for this request: `granted` or `declined`), maps it onto S8's `AppointmentRequestCreate` (path patient, source channel `portal`) and calls S8's own `createRequest` with origin `portal`. The S8 seam: a portal origin requires a portal principal and a portal authority (and a staff call can never carry it); the authority's `authorize` (E1 check for `scheduling`) runs in the command transaction before the idempotency executor, so replays are reauthorized; its `bound` (abuse bounds) runs inside the executor, so a replay returns the original instead of counting again. Everything else (patient schedulable, location active, provider at the location, preferences, transition row, audit, outbox, idempotency) is S8's unchanged code. Bounds (recommended-pending-approval): 3 open or in-review portal requests per patient, 10 portal submissions per principal per 24 hours; refusal 429 `rate_limited` with `detail.reason` and `detail.limit`, plus an attempt row `portal_rate_limited`.

Acceptance cases (`portal-requests.test.ts`, 8 tests): (1) a submission is one S8 request (state open) that the staff read and list return with the same reference, origin `portal`, source channel `portal`; its audit row is `scheduling.requests.create` by principal `portal`; no request table other than S8's exists; the portal requests view shows it `pending`; (2) a same-key resubmission (lost response) returns the original with `replayed: true` and no new row; a changed payload under the key is 409 `idempotency_conflict`; (3) only staff book from the request (the view then shows `scheduled`); cancelling that appointment leaves the request transitions unchanged; a portal token on the staff booking route is 401; (4) a guardian with `scheduling` requests for the minor (subject = minor, origin portal); a proxy holding only `demographics` is 403 `relationship_scope_missing` with an attempt row; an unrelated patient and a random reference give identical 404 bodies; nothing is written; (5) a guardian revoked between a successful page load and the next submit is 403 `relationship_revoked`, and a replay of an earlier successful key is reauthorized and refused the same way.

Forbidden fields, bounds and failure: tenant, patient, urgency, note and source-channel fields are 400 and the marker in a refused note never reaches responses or logs; an unknown location is 404 and a non-provider preference 422 (S8); the fourth open request of one patient is 429 `portal_open_request_limit` with limit `portalOpenRequestsPerPatient = 3` and attempt `portal_rate_limited`; an inactive location is 422 `locationInactive` (the request-window rule until a closure calendar exists); an engagement service without the scheduling aggregate answers 503 `service_unavailable` with `aggregate_unavailable` and stores nothing.

Negative harness: `portal.requests.submit` appended to the portal credential rows (write snapshot unchanged for every refused credential).

- Red: `corepack pnpm test -- tests/integration/portal-requests.test.ts` before implementation: exit 1, 8 failed (8).
- Green: same command exit 0, 8 passed. `typecheck`, `lint`, `contract:check` exit 0; `corepack pnpm test` exit 0, 46 files / 1001 passed / 13 todo.

Deviations: the request-window rule is S8's location status (no closure calendar or configured request windows exist yet); the acknowledgement message is not enqueued (no S8 intent purpose exists for it; E6/E7 deliver S14 intents only); the portal request list is the E2 `requests` view rather than a separate list operation.

Unresolved: closure calendar and request windows; portal free-text notes (refused until E5 messaging and a retention class exist).
